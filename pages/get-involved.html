Donate Food

Have Surplus Food?

Don't let good food go to waste! Whether it's from your restaurant, wedding, office pantry, or home — we'll pick it up, food-safety check it, and ensure it reaches people in need within hours.

📋 What We Accept:
  • ✅ Freshly cooked meals (within 4 hours of cooking)
  • ✅ Raw vegetables and fruits
  • ✅ Sealed / packaged food items
  • ✅ Bakery and dairy products
  • ❌ Expired or spoiled food
  • ❌ Alcohol or non-veg not clearly labeled
