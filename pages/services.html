What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive food rescue and hunger relief programs across 18+ cities.

Service 01

Food Donation Pickup

We collect surplus food from restaurants, hotels, caterers, weddings, canteens, and corporate events. Our trained volunteers arrive within 2 hours of your call.

  • Available 7 days a week, 7AM – 10PM
  • Food safety & hygiene checks at pickup
  • GPS-tracked vehicles for transparency
  • Digital receipt provided to donors
  • Tax exemption certificate (80G) issued
Schedule a Pickup
Service 02

Volunteer Network

Our 1,200+ trained local volunteers are the heart of our operations. From food pickup to distribution, our volunteers ensure meals reach communities quickly.

  • City-wise volunteer coordinator teams
  • Training on food safety and handling
  • Flexible timing — weekend or weekday
  • Volunteer recognition and certification
  • Community and team bonding events
Join as Volunteer
Service 03

Community Kitchens

We operate 8 community kitchens across Telangana and AP that cook and distribute fresh, nutritious meals daily to shelters, slums, and underserved localities.

  • FSSAI-certified kitchen operations
  • 500–1,000 fresh meals per kitchen daily
  • Balanced nutrition with local recipes
  • Priority for children, elderly, and pregnant women
  • Monthly nutrition outcome tracking
Partner a Kitchen
Service 04

Event Food Recovery

Register your wedding, conference, or corporate dinner with us and we'll ensure no food is wasted. Our team arrives before the event ends to pack and redistribute.

  • Pre-event registration & planning
  • On-site packing team provided
  • Redistribution within 3 hours of event
  • Impact certificate for your event
  • Social media feature for your event
Register Event
Service 05

Corporate CSR Programs

Partner with Hack To Feed for your company's CSR initiatives. We offer structured, impactful hunger-relief programs with full impact reporting and employee engagement.

  • Customized CSR program design
  • Employee volunteer engagement events
  • Quarterly impact reports with data
  • Brand visibility on all distribution drives
  • 80G tax-exempt receipts for donations
Become CSR Partner

Process

How It Works

Our streamlined process ensures food is rescued and redistributed in under 3 hours.

📞

1. You Call

Notify us about surplus food via app, call, or WhatsApp.

🚗

2. We Dispatch

Nearest volunteer picks up within 2 hours, food-safety checked.

📦

3. We Pack

Food is sorted, packed hygienically, and dispatched to communities.

❤️

4. Served

Food reaches needy communities. You receive an impact report.
