Who We Are

About Hack To Feed

Our story, our people, and our unwavering commitment to ending hunger.

🎯

Our Vision

A world where no food is wasted and no person goes to bed hungry. We envision a India where surplus food is systematically redistributed to those in need through technology, community, and compassion.

🚀

Our Mission

To reduce food waste and hunger by building an efficient, technology-enabled network of donors, volunteers, and communities — delivering safe, nutritious food to the most vulnerable populations across India.

👨‍💼
Founder's Story

Born from a Single Moment

It was 2019 when our founder, Aakash Mehta, witnessed a hotel discard over 100 kg of food from a wedding banquet while hungry street children sat just outside its gates.

That moment ignited Hack To Feed. Starting with just a WhatsApp group of 12 volunteers and 3 restaurant partners, Aakash began connecting surplus food to the needy in Hyderabad.

Seven years later, Hack To Feed spans 18 cities, has a volunteer army of 1,200+, and has served over 1.5 lakh meals — proving that one moment of conscience can spark a movement.

Our Journey

How We Grew

2019

The Beginning

Founded in Hyderabad with 12 volunteers and 3 restaurant partners. Served the first 100 meals at a local shelter.

🌱
📱
2020

Digital Expansion

Launched mobile volunteer coordination app. Handled COVID-19 pandemic relief — distributed 15,000 meals in 60 days.

2021

City Expansion

Expanded to 5 Telangana districts. Opened first dedicated community kitchen. Grew volunteer base to 300+.

🏙️
🏆
2023

National Recognition

Won Best NGO – Hunger Relief Award. Expanded to 12 cities across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. 50,000 meals milestone.

2026

Pan-India Operations

Now active in 18 cities, 1,200+ volunteers, 350+ restaurant partners, and 1.5 lakh+ meals served.
