Our Mission

We Believe No Food Should
Go to Waste

Every day, tonnes of perfectly good food goes to waste while millions go to bed hungry. Hack To Feed bridges this gap — working tirelessly to rescue surplus food and redirect it to communities that need it most.

We operate a local volunteer network that picks up food from restaurants, weddings, and corporate events, then distributes it to underserved communities across 18+ cities.

What We Do

Our Core Services

From farm to fork, we manage every step of food rescue and redistribution.

🚗

Food Donation Pickup

We collect surplus food from restaurants, caterers, weddings, and events within hours of your call — ensuring fresh, safe food reaches those in need.

🙋

Volunteer Network

Our 1,200+ trained local volunteers act as the backbone of food distribution, reaching communities quickly and efficiently.

🍲

Community Kitchens

We operate community kitchens that cook and distribute freshly prepared meals daily to shelters, slums, and underserved localities.

🎉

Event Food Recovery

Partner with us for your weddings, conferences, and corporate events to ensure leftover food is rescued and redistributed — not wasted.

🏢

Corporate CSR Programs

We help companies fulfill their CSR obligations through structured, impactful hunger-relief partnerships with measurable outcomes.

📊

Impact Reporting

Transparent real-time tracking of every meal served, every volunteer deployment, and every rupee spent — because trust is everything.
